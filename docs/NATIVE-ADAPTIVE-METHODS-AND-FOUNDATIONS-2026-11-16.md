# Native duplex methods and foundations: decision refresh

Reviewed 16 November 2026 against first-party papers, model cards and source. Research only: no model download, GPU job, optimizer update, checkpoint edit, provider call or foundation change was performed by this review. This supplements rather than replaces the existing product goal, critical-review plan and historical Venus/PersonaPlex/Context Spanning work.

## Recommendation

**Keep the current native handoff/perception attribution work first. Add a bounded feasibility check for NVIDIA VoiceChat11B before committing another large PersonaPlex training phase. Keep Context Spanning's asynchronous design, activate genuinely adaptive OPAL in the next admitted training experiment, and use speech-specific interactivity RL plus MiRL's error-targeted replay ideas once their respective state/reward contracts are real. Weight-space editing remains a falsifiable research arm, not the presumed cure.**

No newly reviewed alternative was verified to meet *all* of: downloadable/commercially usable weights, adequate native reasoning, reliable micro-turn behaviour, continued foreground conversation and interruption during tool work, and a supported ARM/GH200 runtime. Do not turn a promising paper or advertised score into an accepted replacement.

### What our evidence actually establishes

The completed [192-output comparison](NATIVE-EXPANDED1072-COMPARISON-2026-11-16.md) used raw PersonaPlex and the105,070,592-coefficient adapter after196 updates. Both had only1/64 quantitative answers without false explanations, zero native RET events, and failed all four quiet and pause cases. Basic answers and strict speech/text agreement improved slightly, with material ASR caveats. The64 questions are arithmetic/quantitative DEV, not a broad-domain reasoning benchmark.

The parent executor additionally reports saved-frame analysis of about37ms per native engine step, p95 below38ms over131,806 frames. That is an execution finding to preserve in its own receipt, not independently remeasured by this research task. It argues against attributing the much slower overall evaluation loop to the foundation's neural-step latency. Native token4 matches the training handoff target; the previous comparison had no connected worker. These observations do not establish that the model learned a useful handoff policy.

## 1. OPAL: use the mechanism, describe its actual extent

The October2026 [authors' report](https://xingjianbai.com/reactive-optimizers/) and [paper2610.33826](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33826) describe a coding agent observing training dynamics and editing optimizer/analysis programs during training. Its scope is richer than choosing an LR multiplier; the authors also investigate update rules, module-specific treatment and numerical guards. They explicitly discuss asynchronous decisions and reproducibility limitations. These are task-specific experimental results, not duplex-speech evidence.

The [actual guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xingjian-bai/opal/main/docs/GUIDE.md) documents rank0 agent decisions, synchronized program changes, snapshots/trials, and checkpoint semantics. `backend="keep"` is a zero-agent equivalence mode. Nonblocking operation keeps the current updater while a decision is pending. Exceptions after partially applied updates are not generally exact rollback; its advertised snapshot/recovery behavior does not replace our full state/restore requirements. Codex backend `max_cost_usd` is not a token/spending cap.

**Our implementation:** the inspected `training/personaplex/adaptive_policy.py` selects factors0.5/0.75/1/1.1 from complete coverage, DEV and retention evidence; `adaptive_boundary.py` carries out a versioned boundary transaction preserving Adam/RNG/update identity. This is a useful local reactive policy over a reviewed catalogue, **not autonomous arbitrary code rewriting**. Source6b32c9d was staged; the completed196-update run remained KEEP. No evidence here says that a new program was applied to that run.

**Next use:** make the next substantive training lineage boundary-adaptive from its start, with the controller's real decision and effective LR recorded. Let current coding-agent analysis propose richer updater/analysis programs between safe boundaries, with disposable execution tests, pinned sources and exact native restores before use. Preserve a fixed-recipe arm when comparing whether adaptation helped. A provider-backed writer remains a distinct connection/payload/cost decision; do not silently activate calls under an API0 phase. Reject a proposal if it changes data, model, objective, fold or update budget under an optimizer-only label. Keep weak/contradictory evidence as an explicit KEEP/defer, not a fabricated improvement.

## 2. MiRL and speech RL solve different parts of the objective

[MiRL, submitted26October2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35697), profiles recurrent terminal-agent misbehaviors, reconstructs prefixes immediately before errors, and trains suffix rollouts. Reward requires both task success **and** avoidance of the targeted mistake. The [paper's implementation details](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35697v1) retain only219 of640 selected prefixes after replay checks. The experiment is on terminal tasks and Qwen3.5-4B, not speech. Its full-weight learner/rollout implementation uses eight A100s; its released recipe should not be pasted into the native speech trainer. [Actual training script](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/runchu-tian/MiRL/main/scripts/train.sh)

**Immediately useful:** an append-only failure queue for distinct TRAIN situations, identified by checkpoint, audio, earliest causal frame, native action, task state and recurrence. DEV may identify a *family* to repair, but copying these exact questions/answers into TRAIN would contaminate the comparison. Supervised failure-family repair is **MiRL-inspired**, not implemented MiRL RL.

**Actual suffix RL needs:** replay equivalence for the native audio/text/KV state, clock, sampler RNG, pending task identities, backend receipts and cancellation status. A transcript alone is insufficient. Require outcome AND behavior rewards: correct answer/result, actual complete handoff, no premature speech/stale result, and a response when one is due. Always-silent policies must not earn a task reward. RL does not require every task already solved; it does require informative reward variation and a faithful rollout/log-probability path. All-zero handoff rewards call for action supervision, an easier curriculum or a competent initial policy before long blind RL runs. The released terminal trainer drops equal-reward groups; that is a useful warning about a degenerate signal, not a native-speech integration.

For micro-turn behavior, the more direct reference is [Multi-Faceted Interactivity Alignment, submitted9June2026/revised28August](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11167): Moshi/PersonaPlex post-training with pause, turn-taking, backchannel and interruption rewards, plus a response-quality reward. This explicitly optimizes interaction outcomes rather than merely token likelihood. The [released PersonaPlex comparator](https://huggingface.co/kyutai/personaplex-rl-seamless) uses GRPO and segments from separate-channel human conversations. Its delta is **CC-BY-NC4.0**, alongside NVIDIA's underlying terms: useful research comparator and method reference, not an assumed commercial product base. Do not infer better general reasoning from interactivity RL.

## 3. Weight-Space Programming: localize a mechanism before editing

The authors' [2November2026 report](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/) supplies Python access and bounded forward evaluation for its Qwen3.5-0.8B experiment. It removes a loop-associated MLP direction, changing131,072 weights; reported MATH-500 rises7.9%→48.1% as frequent long reasoning loops diminish. The non-looping Gemma270M experiment does not establish a comparable gain. Thus the relevant evidence is removal of a particular obstruction, not manufacture of missing reasoning knowledge or tool behavior.

**Proposed arm, only if localized:** on a disposable copy, test whether a stable activation direction or output-head suppression causally explains repeated TRAIN failures. Compare the proposed edit with a sham edit, a matched simple projection/direction-removal baseline and decoding-only controls. Publish exact sparse diff/norm, revertibility, held-out behavior, ordinary retention, native speech and timing. Stop the arm if the effect is not localized or disappears under those controls. Never transplant Qwen directions into Moshi, edit the preserved original checkpoint or count a toy-policy success as a repaired duplex model. No such voice-model edit is implemented or justified yet.

### VoiceChat11B: concrete compatibility check before GPU work

The [released model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-NemotronLabs-VoiceChat-11B) dates the release3August2026. It takes16kHz user audio and emits22.05kHz agent audio, agent text and user transcription, with a separate function channel. Its9B Nemotron backbone makes it a materially different comparator. No Moshi adapter can simply be loaded into it. Its offline function-call demo accepts a prewritten response and **does not execute a live tool**. Official observed benchmark numbers are not our acceptance result.

The [NeMo branch README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/nemotron-labs-voicechat/README.md) exposes native inference/training paths and an80GB-minimum GPU requirement. It documents a **single fixed voice/no voice cloning**, roughly two-minute training context, imperfect arithmetic/reasoning, possible gibberish/self-talk and unreliable function arguments. Critically, it says the user **cannot interrupt during tool execution**. Therefore it does not yet satisfy our continued-foreground/pending-work contract merely because it supports function calls.

The optimized container explicitly requires **x86_64**, so it is not a supported drop-in on ARM GH200. The native Python route may be portable, but ARM Mamba/causal-conv1d/codec dependencies are unverified here. With the current proven pipeline intact: inspect/pin source and dependency wheels on CPU, test contained imports and required small CUDA operators before loading model weights, and abandon the incompatible route at a short predeclared engineering boundary rather than consuming repeated GPU starts. [Official deployment prerequisites](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/nemotron-labs-voicechat/voicechat_realtime_instructions/prerequisites.md)

Training support is substantive, not just a framework name: `duplex_stt_model.py` includes `training_step`, native speech/function labels, `maybe_install_lora`, and `configure_model` with actual FSDP sharding; `s2s_dataset.py` supplies data, and the README describes merging trained STT/TTS/RNNT checkpoints. This does **not** prove our training state/OPAL/retention code is compatible. [Actual model source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/nemotron-labs-voicechat/nemo/collections/speechlm2/models/duplex_stt_model.py) Its overridden `load_state_dict` can catch a strict-load error and retry partial initialization with `strict=False`: independently check every checkpoint key/shape and reject missing/unexpected state before calling a result a pristine baseline. The supplied [combine configuration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Speech/nemotron-labs-voicechat/examples/speechlm2/conf/nemotron-labs-voicechat.yaml) has placeholder data and one-step settings; it is not a ready substantial training recipe.

Weights use OpenMDW1.1; NeMo code uses Apache2.0. OpenMDW grants broad rights without a noncommercial restriction, subject to its notice/distribution and other terms; read the actual text rather than assuming NVIDIA PersonaPlex's license applies. [License](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenMDW/OpenMDW/main/1.1/LICENSE.OpenMDW-1.1)

**GH200 memory:** official80GB inference requirement is below120GB in VRAM capacity, but that does not resolve ARM/runtime compatibility. An11B full-Adam training planning estimate of16bytes per trainable coefficient is176GB *before activations*; this is arithmetic, not a measured requirement, and optimizer precision/offload can change it. Use selective tuning or distributed2–4GPU planning if this foundation wins; do not infer single-GPU full-training feasibility from inference fit. Existing Moshi also has an [official full-finetuning switch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-finetune/main/example/moshi_7B.yaml), so larger LoRA is not the only option. Neither more weights nor a full update fixes a miswired action or bad target automatically.

## 5. Speech reward/scorer lesson worth importing now

Oruk's10November2026 [OrukQwen report](https://oruk.ai/research/orukqwen-rl) distinguishes transcription convention, word-aligned credit, model averaging and true recognition gains; its masked-RL arm does not establish a significant universal credit-rule win. This is directly relevant to our ASR-only terminal “thank you” problem: qualify what counts as an error before optimizing that score. Use a disjoint no-speech/reference transcription control and preserve raw outputs. Do not silently remove inconvenient words or adopt a speech-recognition reward as a naturalness/delegation metric.

## Ranked execution and stop criteria

1. **Finish the connected native check and input attribution.** Pass: an actual native trigger creates an auditable asynchronous task; audio continues while pending; cancellation retires the matching task; only verified nonstale results enter a span. Forced-token unit tests prove wiring only. If sampled RET remains zero, diagnose the policy/supervision separately. Use whole matched cohorts and existing outputs where conditions are unchanged.
2. **Run one bounded new-foundation feasibility/comparison if compatible.** Include correct ordinary answers, spoken number capture, uncertainty/routing, required silence, pause, genuine interruption and delayed/cancelled tool results. Stop a candidate that cannot preserve foreground interaction or fails the official runtime contract. Do not run an endless catalogue.
3. **Select the next substantial learning branch from the cause.** Perception gap→qualified number/entity/phrase contrast data or the relevant alignment modules; true reasoning gap→better verified reasoning/domain data and selective/full LM tuning; action gap→exact native action/context/clock targets. Keep ordinary/speech retention and full held-out behavior. Insufficient adapter capacity is a hypothesis to compare, not a conclusion from105M versus historical698M across different architectures.
4. **Use active OPAL and targeted RL deliberately.** Let complete behavioural/retention evidence control actual versioned updates; compare against a fixed recipe. Use interactivity RL for timing and MiRL-style prefix selection for recurring task/control failures after exact replay/reward tests pass. Stop if reward can be gamed by silence, promised tools or wrong-but-consistent speech.
5. **WSP only after a causal localization.** Stop on failure to beat sham/simple-edit controls or retention damage. Scratch pretraining is not the next default: no present result establishes that it is needed, or that a new random model would be a cheaper path than adapting a competent released foundation.

These are proposed experiments and decision gates, not implemented improvements. The parent executor owns actual source changes, jobs, budgets, acceptance and the live handoff.

## Feasibility appendix: MiniCPM-o4.5 (16 November follow-up)

**Verdict: a justified bounded pristine baseline, not an approved replacement or a drop-in Venus/Moshi continuation.** The released Apache2.0 model combines a Qwen3-8B language backbone with audio/vision/speech components (9B total). This differs from the older Venus adaptation already preserved and rejected in this project. A newer competent text backbone could improve native reasoning; it does not establish improved duplex behaviour or prove our prior Venus failure is fixed. **Actual duplex path, not the half-duplex demo:** the released `MiniCPMODuplex` wrapper reuses the loaded model through `as_duplex()`, maintains a streaming decoder, and exposes `prepare`, `streaming_prefill` and `streaming_generate`. Its defaults are 16kHz input, 1,000ms chunks (1,035ms first chunk); results include listen/speak state, text, waveform and turn ending. The code explicitly describes prefill once per second. This makes subsecond microturn responsiveness a *critical unproven acceptance point*: do not reduce the chunk setting and call the training contract preserved. Audio-only prefill has a separate AUDIO mode, without requiring video frames. Ordinary `chat` genuinely accepts user-role text with no image/audio and applies the chat template, unlike PersonaPlex's question-in-persona-prefix condition. Use this for a real text-versus-audio reasoning control, keeping thinking/decoding settings explicit. But inspected `chat` only accepts system/user/assistant roles, and the duplex source exposes no verified typed tool-call channel or learned action contract. Raw text feeding is **not** demonstrated tool-role semantics, an async scheduler, reliable task selection or stale-result cancellation. Build and qualify these explicitly; never concurrently mutate one decoder from worker threads. Existing RET/action adapters are incompatible. [Source methods and supported arguments](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-o-4_5/blob/main/modeling_minicpmo.py)

**GH200/strict loading:** the documented vanilla path permits SDPA, without mandatory FlashAttention; it tests Python3.10, Transformers4.51 and Torch2.3–2.8. Check CPU imports in an isolated environment before GPU admission, and do not alter the valid native runtime. The 9B BF16 parameter lower bound is approximately18GB; auxiliary assets, KV cache and activation/workspace overhead must be measured. Inference fit does not establish full-Adam training fit. Require one immutable source/model/auxiliary-asset revision, offline resolved assets, complete key/shape/missing/unexpected receipts and exact inference-state hashes before calling the baseline pristine. Utility/vocoder internals and a native training/OPAL restore integration were not verified here. [Asset initialization source](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/MiniCPM-o-4_5/blob/main/modeling_minicpmo.py)

**Bounded go/no-go:** after the existing causal probe, qualify clean loading and ARM dependencies; compare matched text/audio arithmetic and ordinary reasoning, British speech, required quiet/pause and genuine interruption using untouched weights. Stop this route if its native one-second scheduling fails the required microturn behaviour, or if verified async result injection cannot retain foreground context. Advance to substantial data/recipe/capacity experiments only after these results; do not replay completed training or reinterpret a text-only gain as a full product win.

First-party NVIDIA source inspected at `16419a2ef7b98611f58c9df07477d00e5ad61d5f` supports two distinct paths. WAV input is resampled/loaded, normalized to **−24LUFS**, framed/padded through Mimi, then forced into the **agent audio** stream with zero text and sine user tokens. Released `.pt` voice prompts instead load a dictionary with **`embeddings` and `cache`**, replay LM input embeddings and copy the saved LMGen delayed-token cache. A local `{'codes': tensor}` file is not that format. The serialized cache matters to delayed stream state; the official saved-embedding path is an optimization of its WAV-prompt processing, not an arbitrary timbre vector accepted by any engine. Official prompt order is voice→silence→system-tagged persona tokens→silence; server silence count is `int(0.5*frame_rate)` (six frames at12.5Hz), before live input. The code does not establish a special universal required14.72s recording length.
