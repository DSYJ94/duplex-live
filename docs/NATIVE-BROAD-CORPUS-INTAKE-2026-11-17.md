# Broader native corpus intake: concrete proposal

17 November 2026. Research and intake design, followed by the separately recorded CPU staging below. No data is admitted and no synthesis, model inference or training has started from this intake. This complements [the substantial-training decision](NATIVE-REASONING-TRAINING-DECISION-2026-11-17.md); it does not select a tuning method or adopt a model.

## Decision and evidence boundary

**Build one provenance-preserving, three-source intake with a 20,000 distinct-episode goal, beginning with a 2,000-candidate text qualification batch.** Use OASST1 for explanation/conversation breadth, Dolly for grounded instruction diversity, and a separately qualified RuleTaker-derived generator for checkable logic. Add explicit clarification/uncertainty and authentic asynchronous interaction examples; the public text sources do not supply valid native timing or engine traces. The first batch is a production-intake milestone, not another GPU pilot. Scale to 10K–50K only after measuring eligible unique yield, review capacity and audio qualification.

The existing decision reports native quantitative weakness and motivates broader data. Task context identifies 954 numeric targets and 450 top-level exact-rational parent records among the expanded TRAIN1008; there are no domain/route/family tags. Do not count 1008 records as 1008 independent reasoning families. Preserve the retained323 interaction components, expanded105M adapter196, and separate Venus698M lineage. Historical `new_semantic_review_required` planning flags do not overturn subsequently completed audio/semantic qualification. This note identifies missing breadth, not retrospective invalidation. The current four-GPU frozen-native TRAIN collection job8553179 remains untouched.

## One implementable intake

All quantities below are **proposed post-filter goals**, not inventory or guaranteed eligible yield:

| Component | 20K goal | First 2K candidate batch | Intended supervision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Dolly-derived | 6,000 | 800 | Grounded QA, extraction, categorisation, stable basic concepts; short answer plus useful explanation |
| OASST1-derived | 4,000 | 700 | Basic/technical explanation, conversational context, follow-up correction; favour short coherent paths |
| RuleTaker-derived | 4,000 | 300 | Bounded one-to-three-step entailment, explicit negation and qualified unknown; varied rule structures |
| Newly authored, paired with eligible source families | 3,000 | 100 | Missing premise/reference ambiguity, clarification followed by supplied fact, honest unsupported-answer handling |
| New authentic interaction episodes | 3,000 | 100 | Direct-versus-actual-delegate contrasts, worker-result grounding, cancellation/replacement/stale results, micro-turn/silence |

Acquire and inventory the complete selected text assets into a new staging lineage first, without changing any admitted manifest. Run automated text checks over the whole eligible cohort while the bounded first2K qualification batch is reviewed; continue approved-family production toward20K without repeated model starts or serial micro-pilots. Keep Dolly share-alike source/derivative artefacts in a separately attributable partition from the permissively licensed collection. If native collection/synthesis is not yet qualified, the last100 first-batch cases are specifications awaiting recordings, not completed episodes. Use at most one initial selected OASST path per tree. Do not multiply paraphrases to hit the quota: publish source-parent, tree, family, unique-content, turn, audio-hour and replay-exposure counts separately. If English reviewed yield is insufficient, report the shortfall and author new verifiable families; do not dilute filters. Prioritise non-math Dolly/OASST material and remove inspected GSM/math template overlaps. Technical coverage must explicitly include computing/networking, simple algorithms, physical mechanisms and explanation of limitations, not just trivia or math. These sources are candidates for that coverage, not proof it has been achieved.

The first800 Dolly candidates should emphasise answer-in-context tasks and classification, with stable concepts represented; the700 OASST candidates should be stratified by subject and conversational depth rather than merely shortest length. Independently review a stratified200-case opening audit (80 Dolly,70 OASST,30 logic,20 newly authored). This establishes defect categories and filter changes; it does not certify the rest. Require per-target semantic approval or a qualified executable verifier before admission. Record unresolved source/topic gaps and scale only approved families; this is compatible with substantial training once the corpus is ready.

## Leakage, split and provenance contract

1. Freeze the inspected regression/acceptance inventory first, including old GSM8K/math DEV, DEV64, official128 and any inspected acceptance prompts, solutions and source IDs. The local [GSM8K source-pool manifest](../../../docs/NATIVE-GSM8K-SOURCE-POOL-2026-11-07.json) still declares fresh-evaluation decontamination pending. Old results remain historical evidence, not a fresh generalisation test. Never turn failure examples, gold solutions, paraphrases, number swaps or templates from these inspected families into TRAIN.
2. Canonicalise Unicode/whitespace; hash question, supplied context and answer separately and jointly. Check exact IDs/hashes against all known training and inspected regressions. Use token-shingle/MinHash plus entity/number-normalised template matching for near duplicates, including cross-source matches. Borderline matches need semantic review; lexical checks cannot certify absence of contamination or unknown foundation pretraining exposure.
3. Attach `source_dataset/revision/file/hash`, parent/tree/article ID, licence/attribution, `domain`, `task_family`, `route`, `answerability`, proof/evidence, derivation template, split, reviewer/verifier status and audio provenance. Missing legacy tags need conservative grouping, not fabricated family precision.
4. Respect official validation/test exclusions; do not repurpose them into training. Within eligible training material, partition parent/theory/tree/article and semantic-family/template groups before rewriting, branching or synthesis. Hold speakers together for a separate acoustic-generalisation cohort. Cluster paraphrases across datasets before assigning splits.
5. Maintain three declared evaluation views: family-disjoint held-outs from eligible sources; a genuinely source-disjoint fresh authored cohort whose sources/templates never feed training; and the frozen historical regressions. Same-source family hold-out is not source-disjoint. Keep threshold calibration disjoint from final evaluation, and collect native correctness labels separately from desired route labels.

## Concise audio without losing verification

Keep two distinct artifacts per answer: (a) the evidence/reference solution, exact result/units or executable proof and independent judgment; (b) the intended spoken response. Aim for one-to-three short sentences (roughly15–60 words) with the answer first and one useful reason; allow shorter labels or a clarification question. Long source explanations stay in the verification artifact. Source context needed to answer must remain in the user/history input: reject over-budget Dolly contexts or create and independently verify a faithful shorter context with recorded derivation; never silently truncate premises or substitute teacher knowledge. Do not simply truncate them, read chain-of-thought aloud, or remove assumptions, negation, units and essential caveats to meet a word limit. Reverify the spoken rewrite against the original question and evidence; a correct long answer does not certify its summary. Ambiguity variants must actually remove needed information and maintain consistent subsequent answers.

Use natural British wording and pronunciation without stereotyped slang or changing facts. Local TTS is a potential conversion step only: installed availability, voice licence, British accent, numbers/units/negation intelligibility, alignment and native code compatibility are unverified here. Qualify those before bulk audio; use independent listening on stratified clips and transcribed-answer verification. No new paid API payloads are needed or authorised. Never place gold answers in persona/user conditioning. The existing native objective is multimodal; these conversions do not implement the separately unqualified text-only rehearsal objective.

## Interaction retention and admission checks

Keep the existing323 qualified components as a separately reported retention stream. Isolated spoken QA does not teach silence, listening during speech, acknowledgement timing or asynchronous dispatch. New timing episodes need explicit audio masks, turn/end boundaries and genuine user pauses; new delegation episodes need actual engine RET, a complete request, real worker receipt/result, correct task/version IDs and cancellation/staleness behaviour. A text line saying “I will check” is not a worker call. Include short-hard and long-easy contrasts: prompt length cannot supply route or competence labels. Easy direct answers and honest uncertainty must coexist with delegation; do not label all difficult questions RET or invent a confident answer while waiting.

Automate schema/lineage/licence completeness, tree reconstruction, empty/truncated messages, language and PII flags, split collisions, dedup reports, label balance, answer/result/unit checks, proof consistency, rewrite provenance, audio duration/alignment and request/result trace checks. Report domain/route/family and numeric/non-numeric coverage before and after filtering; report all missing/unknown verification fields. Executable checks cover formal tasks only. Human or independent semantic review remains necessary for factual correctness, explanation usefulness, appropriate clarification/delegation, safe unknown semantics, British naturalness and audio meaning. Crowd rank, ASR agreement and a second unqualified model are insufficient substitutes.

Admission requires an immutable manifest and review record, qualified native encoding/audio, frozen disjoint evaluation, and complete retention/control comparisons. This note does not establish any of those gates as passed. It enables preparation of a substantial broader corpus while the valid collection continues; training starts only under the separately reviewed native comparison plan.
