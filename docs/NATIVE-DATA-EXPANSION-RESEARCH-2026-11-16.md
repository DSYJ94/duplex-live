## Gold, proposals and qualification

1. **Verifiable gold:** immutable source fact plus approved minimal explanation; deterministic program output; explicit synthetic world state; or a tool receipt/state oracle. Unit-test the oracle separately from generated wording. An executable answer verifies its result, not every sentence of an explanation.
2. **Local27B is a proposer:** give it only TRAIN source/state and ask for varied questions, concise explanations and natural multi-turn wording. Preserve raw candidates/model/decoding provenance. Self-agreement, another sample from the same model, or its own “correct” rating is not independent gold. Unsupported explanatory claims are rejected or quarantined, not voted into truth.
3. **Independent semantic admission:** exact entity/negation/state/action checks, executable answer tests and source-grounded explanation rubric; independently review every new conceptual template/factual explanation and all ambiguous failures. Template-approved variants still require critical-slot validation. Keep unresolved rows out of TRAIN. Do not copy benchmarks or fresh DEV examples as teaching material.
4. **Local speech admission:** Kokoro0.7.16's actual548-tensor strict CPU gate passed according to the executor; no source audio has yet been generated/admitted. Use pinned British voices and existing local ASR independently of the synthesizer, require exact critical numbers/names/negations/actions, no clipping/empty/extra speech and British intelligibility review across every family/short-utterance stratum. Preserve source and waveform hashes. TTS does not certify factual correctness.
5. **Native admission:** compile real timed user/agent streams and genuine action targets; plain spoken “I'll check” is not RET or a dispatched task. Retain required quiet/pause, pending-result/cancellation clocks and ordinary speech/knowledge rehearsal. Missing native action/cache/restore proof blocks the corresponding learning claim, not all unrelated corpus work.

Freeze every current DEV/held-out source ID, normalized text, parent lineage, question/answer and semantic family before generation; exclude overlapping near-paraphrases and transformations. Split new families before local27B expansion/TTS. Existing held-out fixtures stay held out. Only add a genuinely fresh acceptance set under separate provenance. Never use the same27B to create acceptance gold and then claim success from its unverified judgments.
