# Research and harness continuity during distributed training

The new distributed execution path supplements the existing training system; it does not replace its objectives or research programme.

- OPAL: preserve executable optimizer programs, update counters, diagnostics and RNG for every rank. Current KEEP/API0 remains the documented policy until a separately versioned adaptive recipe is admitted. A checkpoint containing an `opal` field alone does not establish exact executable restore; fresh-process equivalence remains required.
- MiRL: use recurring TRAIN failure prefixes and recovery contrasts after a faithful native rollout/reward environment exists. Do not relabel supervised failure mining as implemented RL or mine inspected evaluation cases into TRAIN.
- Native interaction GRPO: retain the model-family research and plan for waveform-grounded silence/interruption, correct answers, calibrated routing and real worker lifecycle rewards, with SFT rehearsal and speech retention.
- Capacity/CPT/full tuning: preserve the matched broad-adapter control, real block-growth and knowledge-stage comparisons in NATIVE-SUBSTANTIAL-TRAINING-RESEARCH-2026-11-13.md. Additional parameters are not assumed to cure data/perception/runtime defects.
- Weight-space programming: preserve the research as a conditional diagnostic intervention; no unvalidated direct weight surgery is active.

Reuse the existing native depth/streaming parity, independent Adam/RNG/OPAL restore, full-cohort speech/semantic review, asynchronous backend cancellation/stale-result harnesses, British listening preferences and live-latency checks. Preserve old actual histories, teacher targets, KL/hidden retention, fit15, folds, receipts and failed artifacts. CPU distributed fixtures do not replace native CUDA admission or product acceptance.

Canonical plans remain NATIVE-PRODUCT-GOAL-2026-11-07.md, NATIVE-ISAMBARD-SCALE-AND-TRAINING-STRATEGY-2026-11-11.md and NATIVE-RL-AND-RESEARCH-INTEGRATION-2026-11-11.md.
