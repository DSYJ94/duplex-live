# Native duplex reasoning: research sweep and direction

Reviewed 24 October 2026. This extends the [21 October research map](FULL-DUPLEX-RESEARCH-MAP-2026-10-21.md); it does not replace a training approval, change frozen gates, adopt a checkpoint, or report live GPU state.

The intended model retains basic reasoning, instruction following and conversational understanding. It handles easy questions directly and delegates sustained reasoning, current knowledge and tools to an asynchronous backend. While work is pending, it continues listening, can answer an unrelated easy follow-up, and respects silence, correction and cancellation. This is a stronger conversational frontend, not merely a text-to-speech mouthpiece.

## What the primary sources establish

**Thinking Machines explicitly endorses this division of labour.** Its system overview describes an intelligent interaction model that delegates reasoning which cannot be produced instantaneously, remains available to the user, and incorporates background results as they arrive. It states that both models are intelligent. Their micro-turn design uses 200ms chunks and shares conversation context; these are properties of their system, not measured properties of ours. The announcement is a research preview and supplies no directly adoptable training recipe for our adapter. Our application of the design is therefore architectural inference, not a claim that we have reproduced their model. [Official system overview](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/interaction-models/)

**Context Spanning already supplies a suitable native interface.** The frontend emits a retrieval trigger, the backend consumes transcribed context, and returned text enters the running native stream through Context Span prefill. The frontend still generates and interprets the final spoken response; the interface does not enforce faithful quotation or establish reliable cancellation. The authors report 47% whole tool-use pass over 100 FullDuplexBench-v3 scenarios, alongside 73.7% interruption and 96% filler rates under that benchmark's definitions, and acknowledge weaknesses in connected multi-turn conversations. These author results support feasibility while directly limiting production claims. Retain our pinned span interface rather than assuming that a stronger backend alone fixes delivery. [Paper §§3–5 and Table4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33443v1), [official implementation](https://github.com/mindlogic-ai/Context-Spanning)

**MoshiRAG supports selective external reasoning, but result arrival is a separate failure surface.** It separates immediate frontend feedback from backend factuality/reasoning. Its paper measures retrieved-reference correctness separately from final spoken correctness; the latter averages about five percentage points lower. Training targets retrieval within roughly two seconds, and nonlocal GPT-4.1/Tavily comparisons assume a uniform 1.5-second delay because API timing was unstable. The official repository warns about delays above three seconds. Accordingly, published QA and mathematical gains do not validate our real long-running, cancelled or revised tasks. Its ARC-encoded reference path also differs from our raw Context Span path: importing its conditioning weights is not an established repair. [Paper §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.12928v3), [official code and runtime guidance](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-rag)

**Kyutai's current interactivity paper is v2, not the v1 used in our earlier note.** Its four-axis GRPO recipe targets pauses, turn-taking, backchannels and interruption, while a semantic judge preserves response quality. The revision adds human paired evaluation: 700 judgments, analysed at dialogue level rather than treating repeated ratings as independent. It also documents incomplete background-speech generalization and a PersonaPlex safety regression from one corpus, mitigated with a revised reward. The objective uses text-token probabilities; reported training uses 32 H100s, 16 completions per group and 100 epochs. This is valuable evidence for trajectory-level timing objectives after supervised behaviour works, not a cheap drop-in fix. Generic pause metrics can exempt short backchannels and therefore cannot replace our stricter explicit-silence instruction test. [Current v2, §§3–5 and AppendicesA–E](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11167v2), [official June post](https://kyutai.org/blog/2026-06-10-interactivity/)

**Other supplied work informs curriculum and measurement more than immediate replacement.** BayLing's ablation shows why “stops speaking” alone is inadequate: a model that almost never speaks can achieve perfect interruption success. FLM-Audio gives evidence that contiguous textual planning can help native speech intelligence, but requires materially different alignment and staged training. DuplexCascade explicitly supervises waiting, thinking, interruption and backchannels, yet uses streaming ASR–LLM–TTS rather than our native codec graph. Transfer these categories and counterexamples into evaluation; do not copy incompatible clocks, silence weights or checkpoints. [BayLing §4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14528v1), [FLM-Audio §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.02521v3), [DuplexCascade §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.09180v1)

## Implications for this project

These are recommendations inferred from the sources and our recorded failures, not findings demonstrated by the papers for our checkpoint.

1. **Keep basic reasoning and learned delegation together.** The existing [reasoning32 proposal](../evals/fixtures/native-reasoning-delegation-source-proposal-20261023.json) already pairs a direct question (“one badge costs four tokens; two badges?”) with a constrained optimisation task and repeats that basic follow-up while the heavier task is pending. This directly matches the clarified target. Do not convert every substantive utterance into a mandatory backend request.
2. **Train result-dependent speech to depend on an arrived result.** Acknowledgements must contain no guessed answer, private value or unsupported completion claim. Keep actual request, receipt, arrival and spoken-word clocks. A valid backend response followed by an earlier fabricated spoken answer is a frontend/delivery failure, not a successful delegation.
3. **Keep interaction control independently measurable.** “Stay silent until Geronimo” requires no audible acknowledgement within the commanded interval. Also require appropriate cue release, ordinary answer onset and resume, so permanent silence cannot pass. A generic backchannel reward is subordinate to the user's explicit silence instruction.
4. **Separate native policy from runtime enforcement.** A request identifier, revision and cancellation state should determine whether a result may be delivered. Spoken “cancelled” is not an actual cancellation receipt. Such a controller can improve system reliability, but its success must not be reported as proof that the model learned routing or cancellation.
5. **Diagnose with two backend conditions.** First provide a correct result at controlled delays, including after an intervening question, correction or cancellation; then use a real reasoning/tool backend. Require the complete scenario family in each condition. Record correct-direct rate, unnecessary delegation, missed delegation, premature-answer rate, returned-answer fidelity, stale-result acceptance, pending-follow-up success, silence compliance and meaningful-answer latency. Do not replace these with time to first filler.
6. **Use a new bounded repair from the retained parent after readiness passes.** Preserve original objectives, voice quality and all existing approvals. The failed control40 candidate is evidence against extending that candidate unchanged. Neither its failure nor four reused development questions isolates batch size, a particular loss coefficient or adapter capacity as the cause. The [base4 correction](NATIVE-CONTROL40-BASE4-SCORING-CORRECTION-2026-10-24.json) also prevents the preliminary 3/4 mechanism-mention count from becoming a misleading model-selection result.

The architecture remains well aligned with the goal. The unresolved task is demonstrating that its native interaction and delegation policy works reliably; changing the goal to “mouthpiece” would hide rather than solve the observed premature-answer and silence failures. No external dataset, model weights or benchmark samples were imported for this review, and no compute, API-generation job or purchase was launched by it.
