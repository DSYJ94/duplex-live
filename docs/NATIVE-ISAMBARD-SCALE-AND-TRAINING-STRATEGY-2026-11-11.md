## Research findings and limits

**Full native LM tuning is a real implementation option.** Kyutai's official configuration exposes full fine-tuning, and its model wrapper enables all LM parameters and uses FSDP FULL_SHARD for multiple GPUs. The official trainer also documents stereo dialogue preparation and multi-GPU launch. This establishes an upstream route, not drop-in compatibility or measured memory/throughput for our PersonaPlex/Context Spanning objectives. Mimi codec training is a separate choice; do not assume every audio component must be unfrozen. [Official configuration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-finetune/main/example/moshi_7B.yaml), [FSDP/model implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-finetune/main/finetune/wrapped_model.py), [official guide](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-finetune/blob/main/README.md).

**Do not assume LoRA is inherently the bottleneck.** Thinking Machines reports that well-configured high-rank LoRA can match full fine-tuning in its tested post-training settings; layer coverage, learning rate and batch size matter, and larger batches can hurt LoRA differently. The supervised comparisons largely use log loss, not native spoken behavior. Our inference: include a properly configured broad-adapter control alongside full tuning, use distinct LR choices, and decide from generated voice/reasoning/control—not parameter count alone. These text experiments do not certify a voice recipe. [LoRA Without Regret](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/lora/).

**Mid-training can add knowledge without restarting pretraining.** Ibrahim et al. demonstrate continual pretraining with LR re-warming/re-decay and replay, including a10B text-model experiment. Their result supports a retention-controlled knowledge phase, not immediate transfer to a tightly coupled speech model. Our inference: use verified technical/basic-reasoning corpora only if competence tests show a knowledge gap; retain native speech/ordinary-history objectives to test and limit speech-space drift. [Continual pretraining paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08763).

**Adding parameters is mathematically legitimate, but supplies no knowledge by itself.** Net2Net demonstrates function-preserving width/depth transfer, originally in vision. LLaMA Pro expands a7B text model to8.3B and trains new blocks on code/math. This is evidence for a conditional block-expansion branch, not proof that it preserves Moshi's streaming caches, acoustic behavior or latency. Our inference: a roughly+0.5–1.5B block-growth candidate is reasonable if matched training identifies a capacity limit. Initially preserve the old function and old blocks; then train new blocks on actual data and test retention/latency. [Net2Net](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05641), [LLaMA Pro](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02415).

**More audio must also have the right interaction structure.** A2026 Moshi-based Japanese-dialogue study reports that corpus turn-taking profiles affect rhythm, high-quality audio improves perceived naturalness, and task-mismatched mixing can harm coherence. Its language/task setting differs from ours. Our inference: expand aligned two-channel conversations with intentional overlaps, gaps, silence, interruptions and appropriate acknowledgements; isolated TTS sentences alone are not a complete duplex curriculum. [Primary paper](https://takamichi-lab.github.io/assets/pdf/paper/2026/abe26iwsds_moshi-corpus-properties.pdf).

**The hardware supports a larger programme.** Official Isambard-AI Phase2 specifications give four GH200 superchips per node,96GB GPU memory per superchip, ARM64 CPUs and Slingshot interconnect. The official distributed tutorial covers multi-node launch with srun/PyTorch/NCCL. Start with one four-GPU node for compatibility, then use multiple nodes when useful measured throughput warrants it. Do not treat aggregate GPU memory as automatically available to one process. [Specifications](https://docs.isambard.ac.uk/specs/), [distributed training](https://docs.isambard.ac.uk/user-documentation/tutorials/distributed-training/).


Use `workq` for sustained batch training. The official scheduling page reserves `interactive` for short interactive/debugging work, not large-scale batch consumption, and distinguishes resource limits from the award's hour balance. Confirm actual association/QoS limits before requesting8–16GPUs; the portal budget alone does not prove concurrency. [Scheduling and reservation rules](https://docs.isambard.ac.uk/user-documentation/information/job-scheduling/).

### Mid-training and added capacity

If the chosen base cannot answer basic questions even under its correct native interface, create a staged knowledge corpus: verified arithmetic/units, causal/basic concepts, technical explanations, contextual reasoning, calibration and routing. A possible preparation range is0.1–1B clean text tokens, then larger only if new evidence supports it. This is a planning range, not available data or a throughput forecast. Keep benchmark test answers and inspected held-out cases out of TRAIN. For a speech model, text-only mid-training must be followed by native audio alignment and complete speech/control retention tests; knowledge gain with degraded speech is not success.

If verified knowledge/data/recipe interventions saturate and additional capacity helps TRAIN and unseen behavior, allocate the growth bucket to an identity-initialized residual/block expansion. Measure additional trainable and total parameters from actual tensor shapes. Keep original weights and histories, give new tensors explicit optimizer/RNG metadata, and test streaming state, depth behavior and live latency. A billion new parameters is a viable experiment, not a mandatory destination. Full foundation pretraining from scratch is not the recommended use of this award.
