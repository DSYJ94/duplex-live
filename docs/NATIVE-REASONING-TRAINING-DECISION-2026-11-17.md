## Decision from current evidence

**Prepare a broad, data-rich native SFT comparison with functional retention, then compare broad adaptation against selective and full native-LM tuning. Do not make another larger adapter or block expansion the default next intervention.** In parallel, build a correctly labelled competence-routing calibration set; training RET emission and estimating native answer failure are different problems.

## Primary-source findings that change the design

**Preserving text competence needs a training objective, not just a pretrained starting point.** Moshi predicts aligned text before audio and mixes text-only batches during pretraining. Its ablation removing the50% text-only mixture lowers spoken-QA accuracy; the authors explicitly find that warm-starting Helium alone is insufficient for retention. They also identify an oral-style/syntactic coverage mismatch. Our inference: preserve the native streaming geometry, but add qualified linguistic/knowledge rehearsal and varied question structures instead of relying exclusively on old hidden/logit anchors or more TTS arithmetic. The50% ratio is evidence from their pretraining, not a validated ratio for our SFT. [Moshi, sections4.4/5.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.00037v2).

**Speech alignment can preserve a competent text core.** Freeze-Omni keeps its LLM frozen while training speech interfaces in stages and adds chunk-wise state prediction for duplex control. This supports separation of linguistic competence, acoustic rendering and interaction objectives; it does not prove an arbitrary new text model can replace Moshi's trunk without alignment, or that its released system meets our timing/accent requirements. [Freeze-Omni, sections2.2–2.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00774v1).

**A stronger linguistic core need not imply scratch audio pretraining.** LLaMA-Omni2 uses pretrained Qwen2.5 instruction models and staged speech-module training on200K synthesized multi-turn dialogues. This is useful evidence for targeted multimodal alignment/data scale, not proof of our required simultaneous listening, interruption, cancellation or backend behaviour. Qwen2.5-Omni separately assigns text generation to Thinker and speech-token generation to Talker, with shared representations. Its reported speech/text instruction performance motivates matching answer competence across modalities, not equating streaming output with accepted duplex interaction. [LLaMA-Omni2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02625v1), [Qwen2.5-Omni](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.20215v1).

**A failure-risk readout needs failure labels.** Local or Escalate? freezes a duplex model and predicts its native answer failures from hidden states available at answer onset; thresholds are calibrated separately from evaluation. Our current head instead alters the internal RET logit at per-frame supervised token positions. The paper gates at the native listen-to-speak/answer-onset boundary using pooled context features; it is neither that temporal RET policy nor a replication of our head. A separate empirical TRAIN correctness-labelled probe could support implicit escalation; reference answers, correctness judgments and future speech must never be inference features. Report actual expert-call rates, native/expert accuracy and time to first answer audio together. Do not assume their selected layer transfers to our model. This would be an explicitly different routing-architecture arm: preserve the current only-actual-engine-RET dispatch contract and do not silently force RET or dispatch a worker inside the existing comparison. [Primary paper, sections3/A/B](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01639v1).

**Full tuning is a comparison, not an assumed cure.** Thinking Machines' tested settings show that sufficiently broad LoRA can match full tuning, with method-sensitive learning rates and batch sizes. This argues against comparing both methods at the same copied LR and calling a difference “capacity.” Its text experiments do not certify native speech retention. [LoRA Without Regret](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/lora/).

**Text replay is not implemented by our current native objective.** `NativeDispatch` and `matched_losses.recorded_text` call `forward_codes` on recorded multi-stream code tensors, masks and streaming-call partitions. They do not currently expose a declared general text-only corpus objective. A replay branch must explicitly implement and qualify the original model's text-mode token/position/audio-null semantics, sequence masks, gradients and restore state; its token budget/exposure must be reported separately. Putting the user's question or a gold answer into the persona/assistant stream would be a conditioning leak, not Moshi-style text rehearsal. Pending that implementation, qualified spoken knowledge/ordinary-context rehearsal is useful but must be labelled native multimodal SFT, not text-only pretraining. [Our dispatch](../training/personaplex/distributed_native.py), [recorded native loss](../training/personaplex/matched_losses.py).

## Concrete next substantial experiment — proposed, not submitted

If the scalar control matches the contextual head, prefer a calibration explanation. If the head fits TRAIN but cannot issue useful native requests, address history/exposure and request construction. 2. **Qualify a genuinely broader corpus before prolonged tuning.** Target10K–50K distinct multi-turn episodes as a collection goal, not an existing asset count. Include basic concepts, context/coreference, arithmetic/units, algorithms and technical explanations, uncertainty, clarification, direct-versus-delegate contrasts, complete request arguments, delayed grounded results and stale/cancel/replacement cases. Maintain separate coverage/exposure counts for reasoning, acoustic fidelity and interaction timing. Reuse existing qualified audio/generators and local British synthesis; no new paid provider payloads are authorized by this note. Isolated spoken Q&A is not a timing curriculum.
3. **Separate competence calibration from desired action supervision.** Collect actual TRAIN-only native answer rollouts with only the source question/audio supplied. Independently label correctness after generation, retaining incorrect/insufficient-context outcomes and authentic request/result traces. Fit a competence-risk probe against observed native correctness, with easy/direct and benign-negative examples; calibrate thresholds on a disjoint family/source calibration split. Never mine the inspected DEV64 or old acceptance cases into TRAIN. Hold out problem families, paraphrase templates and speakers together; a rewritten DEV question is still leakage.
4. **Run a substantial matched learning comparison, not another foundation catalogue.** Common new corpus/exposure and unchanged native inference: A broad105M adaptation; B a precisely counted selective shared-trunk/text-head update with acoustic decoder and Mimi frozen initially; C full native LM tuning with Mimi frozen. Selective tuning is a proposed implementation, not an existing admitted mode. Start within-seed method comparisons from the same released foundation. Preserve196 as a fixed reference; comparing a196 warm start against a fresh full-tune seed would confound method and starting function. Reusing196 as a common seed requires a separately verified function-preserving parameterization, not a casual BF16 LoRA merge.
5. **Use mixed supervision and functional retention.** Keep native text/audio alignment and measured timing masks, add correct concise answer/uncertainty/action targets and linguistic rehearsal, and score ordinary/native reasoning and speech alongside control. Inspect gradient/objective scale and functional retention rather than preserving a large old hidden-loss coefficient by tradition. Compare method-appropriate, predeclared recipes; bounded selection then a full admitted epoch/exposure, not perpetual microtests.

## Memory, OPAL and stop criteria

Our [full-LM implementation](../training/personaplex/distributed_native.py) casts trainable LM weights toFP32 and usesFSDP. Illustrative7B FP32 weights+gradients+two FP32 Adam moments require16bytes/parameter, or112GB decimal **before** activations, BF16 compute materializations, transient gathers and buffers. Four-way ideal state sharding reduces that portion to28GB/rank; it does not prove fit or throughput. Count the actual whole LM, including its acoustic/depth components, and measure a representative full native batch. Use a justified four-GPU node first; scale8/16 only when measured useful throughput warrants it. Do not assume a96GB device holds this full training state or silently replace the existing optimizer with an unverified low-bit/offload route.

OPAL may propose executable optimizer changes only at backed, evaluated new-phase boundaries, against a fixed control; it cannot repair missing labels automatically. MiRL-style recurrent-TRAIN-failure mining is useful immediately, but is not RL. Native RL follows a competent supervised seed and requires variable valid rewards, policy log probabilities, genuine worker receipts and replayable audio/KV/RNG/task state. Weight-space surgery remains a reversible localized-pathology experiment; new parameters or sparse edits do not create missing knowledge on their own.

Stop or change the branch when complete TRAIN cannot fit despite valid updates; when TRAIN improves but fresh native reasoning/control regresses; or when speech/latency deteriorates enough to erase task gains. Respond respectively with objective/coverage or selective/full-capacity comparison, data/retention repair, or acoustic/interaction separation. Only consider continued pretraining or function-preserving growth after the evidence identifies missing knowledge or persistent representational underfit. None of these papers establishes which method will win for our checkpoint; complete native task, speech, backend and live acceptance remains required.
