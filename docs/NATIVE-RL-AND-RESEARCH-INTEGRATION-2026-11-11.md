## Decision and relationship to the product

**Add native interaction and reasoning-routing RL after the current foundation comparison and a coherent supervised seed.** RL is useful because token prediction alone does not directly optimize when to speak, whether to delegate, or whether cancelled work stays cancelled. It does not require all behaviors to be solved in advance. It does require reliable rewards, faithful environments and enough useful exploration to distinguish better actions. If every sampled answer is gibberish or every request is invalid, repair the seed/data/interface first rather than spending an entire RL run on an uninformative reward.

Keep basic reasoning and appropriate delegation as separate requirements: answer simple questions correctly, recognize uncertainty, route genuinely difficult work with complete context, and accurately speak the returned result. A model that delegates every basic question or invents a worker result is not qualified. RL is an additional learning stage, not a reason to discard broader supervised data, mid-training, full fine-tuning or conditional capacity expansion.

DeepSeek-R1 is primary evidence that reasoning can emerge through verifiable-reward RL without an initial SFT stage, but its authors also report readability/language problems in R1-Zero and introduce cold-start/multi-stage training. That text-model result supports investigating RL; it does not establish that our small native voice corpus can reproduce frontier reasoning. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948), [author implementation overview](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1#1-introduction).

## Reward contract to implement

These are proposed requirements, not executable rewards or measured results. Predeclare scoring/thresholds on DEV before final evaluation. Keep a vector of raw task, speech, timing and interaction measures; a scalar reward must not conceal a critical failure.

| Axis | Evidence and positive outcome | Necessary counterexample / protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic native reasoning | Correct concise answer from actual recognized/emitted content; deterministic value/unit checks where appropriate; native-versus-text control diagnoses perception errors. | Fluent wrong answers, guessing unverifiable facts, or delegating every trivial question receive no task success. |
| Appropriate delegation | Valid request, complete relevant context, genuine worker start/receipt, correct grounded result, with easy-direct/hard-delegate/uncertain-clarify contrasts. | Tool syntax or saying “I will check” alone earns no completion. Measure unnecessary calls and wrong direct answers separately. |
| Cancellation and pending work | Real pending worker, cancellation event acknowledgement in state, no later delivery of cancelled/stale results; ordinary conversation continues when permitted. | Immediate malformed-request errors do not exercise cancellation. Include races, late completions and multiple task identities. |
| Required silence / cue release | Actual waveform has no speech/backchannel before the required cue; after the real cue, an appropriate timely response occurs. | “What is the keyword?”, “uh-huh” and all-silent-after-cue policies fail. Include cue quoted hypothetically and explicit rule changes. |
| Pause, interruption and turn-taking | Native timestamps and emitted speech show correct stopping/resumption and content relevant to the new user input. | Rewarding speed alone encourages interruption or meaningless fragments. Ordinary conversational pauses differ from mandatory keyword silence. |
| Natural voice / accent | Intelligibility, text/audio consistency, raw peak integrity, approved British/RP listening preference and appropriate acknowledgements. | No gain reduction to hide clipping; no accent proxy treated as intelligibility; no reward for backchannels while explicit silence is active. |

Use verified task completion **and** avoidance of the target behavior for MiRL success. Preserve existing hard qualification thresholds; an invalid rollout must not receive a high successful reward just because it is fast or silent. Record all attempted rollouts, invalid reasons and costs, not just passing samples. If a GRPO group has no usable reward difference, report it and sample better TRAIN tasks/repair the seed; never manufacture advantages or silently drop the failed family from evaluation.

Semantic/naturalness judges must be calibrated against verified positives, subtle wrong answers, gibberish, all-silence and unnecessary delegation. Prefer local verifiers and locally hosted authorized judges for this API0 programme. An LLM judge is auxiliary evidence, not the sole truth oracle. New external speech/judging payloads require their applicable authorization; this document sends none.

For preference training, first verify that saved ratings identify the exact input/output hashes, blind A/B mapping, score scale, rater and recording version. Derive pairs only when same-context preferences are meaningful and basic correctness holds. A browser dropdown or unverified rating count is not yet an admitted DPO corpus.
