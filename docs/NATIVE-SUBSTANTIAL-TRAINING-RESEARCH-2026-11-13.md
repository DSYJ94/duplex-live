## What our evidence permits

The [completed192 comparison](NATIVE-MOSHI-COMPLETE192-DECISION-2026-11-13.md) establishes actual adapter learning and useful speech-fidelity gains for PersonaPlex105M, but no successful silence/pause condition or qualified reasoning/delegation product. It does not compare a7B backbone against a larger backbone or full tuning. Its small, already-inspected regression cohort cannot diagnose a total-parameter ceiling. Both base errors and candidate regressions are present; the rope/knot outputs also leave an acoustic-perception confound. This makes a substantive matched experiment more informative than either declaring LoRA sufficient or scaling every component blindly.

The prepared1008TRAIN text curriculum is a seed. It includes512 authored arithmetic examples from16 templates plus496 public GSM8K TRAIN rows. It is not1008 independent capability families, a broad technical corpus or already-qualified native audio. Neither repeated versions of the same323 native components nor arbitrarily long training over those components supplies the coverage needed for the next programme.

## Verified implementation routes and their limits

**Full tuning already has an upstream Moshi route.** The official example exposes `full_finetuning`, requires disabling LoRA and adapter-only saving for that mode, and enables activation checkpointing. The model wrapper makes every loaded LM parameter trainable in the full branch and uses FSDP `FULL_SHARD` for multiple GPUs. This proves an upstream implementation option, not compatibility with our PersonaPlex checkpoint, Context Spanning overlay, retained objectives or state format. Full LM tuning does not automatically mean retraining Mimi; keep the codec frozen initially. The official data interface uses aligned two-channel audio plus timed transcripts, so a generic text-only trainer is not automatically a native duplex trainer. [Configuration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-finetune/main/example/moshi_7B.yaml), [actual model/FSDP code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-finetune/main/finetune/wrapped_model.py), [data and launch guide](https://github.com/kyutai-labs/moshi-finetune/blob/main/README.md).

**Retain the adapter control and tune recipes fairly.** Thinking Machines finds that sufficiently broad/high-rank LoRA can match full tuning in its tested post-training settings. Layer coverage, batch size and learning rate matter; its optimal full-tuning LR was substantially lower than the LoRA LR. Its main supervised evidence is loss, with additional math-RL evaluations, not native speech. Our inference: compare a properly tuned broad adapter against full tuning, rather than copying one LR/batch recipe into both and declaring capacity the cause. [LoRA Without Regret](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/lora/).

**Continued pretraining is a credible knowledge intervention.** Ibrahim et al. report that LR re-warming, re-decay and replay support continued learning, including a10B text-model experiment, more efficiently than restarting the training corpus. This does not certify speech-space preservation. Our inference: use a separately versioned general/math/technical corpus with replay, then the same native SFT alignment as the other arms. Retain speech/control objectives during adaptation and check acoustic drift; do not assume text-loss improvement establishes spoken reasoning. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08763).

**Growth is mathematically sound, with architecture-specific work.** LLaMA Pro expanded7B to8.3B by adding initially identity-like Transformer blocks and training new capacity on code/math. Its construction carefully handles residual outputs; blindly zeroing a normalization scale can kill gradients. It is evidence that a pretrained model can gain capacity without starting over, not a Moshi streaming proof. Our inference: implement a roughly+0.5–1.5B block-growth arm, count actual tensors, and verify initial function, live streaming/KV state, delayed text/audio streams, causality, gradients and restores. New weights initially contain no additional knowledge and extra sequential depth may increase latency. [Paper and method](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.02415v2).

## Concrete next experiment

Use untouched PersonaPlex as the common retention reference and proposed learning seed; preserve the105M candidate and all Context Spanning/Venus states. Keep a separate connected-CS system comparison so a disconnected request interface is not mistaken for a complete backend. Foundation adoption remains open.

| Arm | Intervention | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| A | Broad/high-rank adapter, expanded native SFT corpus | How far better data and recipe can take efficient adaptation |
| B | Full native LM fine-tuning, same corpus/exposure | Whether restricted trainable updates constrain learning |
| C | Exact-count function-preserving block expansion, same SFT exposure | Whether more total representational capacity helps |
| D | Continued pretraining on a real knowledge corpus, then the same SFT | Whether missing knowledge or representation adaptation is the constraint |

A/B/C should share corpus versions, folds, starting function, evaluation inputs and declared data exposure, while allowing method-appropriate LR/batch choices. Report equal-exposure comparisons and actual cost/latency separately. D deliberately changes training data/stage: compare it against its own no-CPT control rather than attributing its improvement solely to parameters. If affordable, repeat the leading comparison with another seed. A small predeclared LR comparison is useful; an open-ended kernel or hyperparameter sweep is not.

Build data preparation and the distributed learner concurrently. Target10k–50k genuinely varied dialogue/routing episodes as an initial collection target, not an asset claim. Include correct concise answers, technical concepts, uncertainty, direct/clarify/delegate contrasts, complete task context, genuine worker receipts, cancellation/stale-result handling, silence/release and interruptions. Use approved British/RP voice preferences with aligned stereo dialogue; isolated TTS is not complete duplex interaction data. Keep fresh families, wording and speakers for evaluation. Do not use the inspected32 as new TRAIN material.

After a coherent seed, add native interaction/reasoning-routing GRPO and MiRL-style repeated-TRAIN-failure practice as already specified in the [RL plan](NATIVE-RL-AND-RESEARCH-INTEGRATION-2026-11-11.md). Verifiable correctness, actual task lifecycle and waveform timing rewards matter more than increasing the number of rewarded rollouts. OPAL can version recipe changes at checkpoint boundaries; it does not replace a correct objective or certify a code rewrite.

## Resource and token-budget discipline

Start the full-LM implementation on a four-GPU node, then measure whether8 or16 GPUs improve useful throughput. Isambard Phase2 uses four96GB GH200 GPUs per node with ARM64 hosts; aggregate memory is not one unsharded device. Use FSDP/optimizer sharding, activation checkpointing and compatible pinned containers; exact memory and interconnect throughput remain to be measured on our full native objective. [Official specifications](https://docs.isambard.ac.uk/specs/).

Record distinct counts for unique examples/families, licensed text tokens, audio hours, native frames/codebook targets, optimizer updates and repeated exposures. At12.5Hz, one hour corresponds to45,000 temporal frames, not45,000 total text-plus-codebook targets. Measure sustained **valid training frames/sec and text tokens/sec at the whole allocation**, including forward/backward, optimizer, retained losses, data loading and checkpoint/evaluation overhead. Then compute `wall_seconds = target_units / measured_allocation_units_per_second` and `GPU_hours = wall_seconds * GPU_count / 3600`, adding measured evaluation/rollout and shutdown reserves. Do not forecast native training from text-only peak FLOPs or multiply throughput by GPU count twice.

The owner's screenshot implies about251.66GPUh/day, roughly10.49GPUs continuously. A productive12–16GPU programme with training, rollout and qualification work can meet that order of usage; actual association/concurrency limits and remaining allocation must be checked live. Use normal `workq` for sustained batch work; the official interactive reservation is for short work and carries a premium. Resume from saved states across scheduler walltime, not duplicated optimizer updates. [Official scheduling rules](https://docs.isambard.ac.uk/user-documentation/information/job-scheduling/).

**From-scratch pretraining remains a separate research option, not the recommended product route.** The award may support meaningful small-scale pretraining experiments; it would be incorrect to declare all scratch training impossible. But reproducing a useful native speech foundation also requires the corpus, speech alignment, tokenizer/codec and distributed implementation. The current evidence supports preserving learned capabilities and testing data-rich full tuning, CPT and growth first. Exact token budgets and GPU-hour envelopes should be fixed from the admitted corpus and one representative throughput measurement, not invented to consume the award.
